About

I care about making work clearer, more consistent, and easier to execute.

My career has been built in environments where priorities change quickly and execution matters. That has taught me how to lead people, manage competing demands, maintain standards, and make practical decisions without losing sight of the customer or the team.

I am especially interested in the systems behind good operations: staffing, communication, accountability, inventory, process design, technology, and the small improvements that reduce friction over time.

This site is my long-term professional home base. It is meant to document where I've been, what I can do, and how my capabilities develop over time—not to force my career into one narrow job title.

Direction

Operations + technology is the most useful path forward.

I have real operating experience, and I do not want to throw that away to chase a completely unrelated technical identity. The more realistic path is to build technical capability on top of what I already know: people, execution, service, inventory, staffing, accountability, and business operations.

That can lead toward stronger operations leadership, technology-enabled operations, implementation, systems support, process improvement, or other roles where understanding both the business and the tools behind it creates an advantage.

I do not need to know the exact future title yet. The priority is becoming more capable at the intersection of operations and technology so that more credible options are available when the right opportunity appears.
