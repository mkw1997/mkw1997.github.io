About

I care more about useful capability than a perfect title.

My background is in retail operations and leadership. I have spent years working in environments where priorities change quickly, people need clear direction, and the quality of execution shows up immediately. That experience shaped how I think about work: understand the system, find the friction, and improve what actually matters.

How I work

Practical first.

I tend to look for the operating reality behind a problem. What is actually happening? Where is information getting lost? What is creating repeat work? What depends too heavily on one person remembering everything?

That mindset applies to staffing, customer service, inventory, training, technology, and almost anything else. I am interested in solutions that make the work clearer and more reliable—not complexity for its own sake.

Principles
